Chatly Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chatly ("Chatly", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Chatly Discord application.

1. Information We Process

Chatly may process information provided by Discord that is necessary to provide its features within servers where Chatly is installed.

This may include:

  • Discord user IDs and server IDs
  • Discord role information
  • Server membership information
  • Discord presence information when required for presence-based features
  • Message content when required for moderation or reporting features
  • Information entered into Chatly features such as profiles or economy features

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide and operate Chatly's Discord functionality, including server management, role management, moderation, reporting, profiles, support systems, engagement features, and other community features.

We do not sell user information or use privileged Discord data for unrelated purposes.

3. Message Content

Chatly may process message content when necessary for moderation, reporting, or related community-management functionality.

Chatly does not use message content to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.

4. Presence Information

Chatly may use Discord presence information for features such as automatic vanity or status-based role functionality.

Presence information is used to provide the relevant Discord server functionality and is not intended to be used as a general activity tracking system.

5. Data Storage

Chatly's current implementation does not store user profile or economy information in an external database. Features that require persistent storage may be introduced in the future.

If persistent user data is introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant data, its purpose, retention, and deletion process.

6. Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of applicable stored Chatly data through Chatly's data-deletion functionality or by contacting the Chatly support team through the server's support system.

Deleting stored data may permanently remove information associated with Chatly features, including applicable profile, economy, progression, XP, level, activity, or other stored data.

7. Data Retention

Chatly retains information only for as long as it is necessary to provide the applicable functionality or satisfy applicable operational requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

Chatly operates through Discord and may rely on Discord's services and infrastructure to provide its functionality. Discord's own privacy practices are governed by Discord's policies.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by Chatly. Security practices may change as Chatly's infrastructure and storage systems develop.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Chatly's functionality, data handling, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.

11. Contact

For privacy or data-deletion requests, users can contact the Chatly staff team through the support system in the Chatly Discord server.
